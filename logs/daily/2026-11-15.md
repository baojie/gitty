## Why it matters

**Why do this?**

Gitty was in daily use with many repositories open at once, which exposed how the tab set, the recent list and closing behaved at that scale.

**What problem does it solve?**

A stray close lost a dozen tabs, the recent menu was capped and unsearchable, and Windows users could not install from source without Visual Studio.

**What does it affect?**

The 0.2.2 packages, the source install on Windows, and every multi-repository session.
